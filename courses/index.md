# Welcome to oss-learn

> Learn how real-world open-source projects are built — by rebuilding them yourself, step by step.

---

## How It Works

Each course takes a real project (like [pi.dev](https://github.com/earendil-works/pi)), distills it down to its core concepts, and guides you through building your own version from scratch. You'll learn architecture decisions, trade-offs, and patterns — not just copy-paste tutorials.

1. **Pick a course** — each one has a clear difficulty level and time estimate
2. **Work through milestones** — each milestone leaves you with something runnable and tangible
3. **Think before you code** — instructions give you requirements and hints, not full solutions
4. **Verify as you go** — concrete checkpoints so you know when you've actually got it working

## Philosophy

??? abstract "Learn by building"
    Passive reading doesn't stick. You write code, you learn it.

??? success "Milestones over monoliths"
    Every milestone leaves you with something working. Progress is visible and motivating.

??? tip "Hybrid instructions"
    Requirements + hints + structural reference patterns. Enough guidance to not spin your wheels, enough challenge to actually think.

??? example "Expert curation"
    These aren't auto-generated. Each course is crafted by someone who understands the project deeply.
